# 30-Day GrokBot GTM Rollout

Advance on evidence, not calendar dates. If a success criterion is not met, repeat the week.

## Week 1 - Trust
Build: Chief of Staff, Meeting Prep, Call Follow-Up Drafter. Routines: none. Run manually. Correct every issue. Put repeated corrections into the bot spec. Success criterion: 10 consecutive outputs you would use unedited.

## Week 2 - Reliability
Build: Signal Watcher and List QA. First routines: Meeting Prep on weekday mornings and Signal Watcher during business hours. Make `workspace/voice.md` and `workspace/signals.md` load-bearing. Success criterion: routines run for seven days without manual verification of whether they actually ran.

## Week 3 - Coverage
Build: Overnight Prospecting, Deal Desk, CRM Hygiene Auditor. Begin saving repeatable methods as Skills. Success criterion: work is routed by bot name and the prospecting queue is consistently cleared.

## Week 4 - Leverage
Build: one Account Expert for the most important account, Account Media Rundown, Fleet Auditor. Run the first fleet audit and delete zombie routines. Success criterion: every remaining routine has produced an output that was acted on within 14 days.

## Day 30 scorecard
1. How many measured hours did this save?
2. Which single bot produced the most value?
3. Which bots have I stopped reading?
4. What share of usage went to monitoring versus generation?
5. What recurring process should enter the Task -> Skill -> Routine ladder next?
